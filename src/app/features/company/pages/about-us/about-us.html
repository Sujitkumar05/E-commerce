OUR PURPOSE

A Cultural Lifeline for Diaspora Communities

Your Store was founded by members of the global Indian diaspora who experienced the exact same longing: the impossibility of finding authentic, unadulterated regional specialties like Vellanki Foods Ariselu, Pulla Reddy sweets, fresh cold-pressed oils, pure organic turmeric capsules, and genuine homeopathic mother tinctures in local overseas grocery stores.

We created Your Store to revolutionize how authentic goods travel across borders. By partnering directly with master makers, rural cooperatives, and authorized pharmacies, we eliminate middlemen, shorten the supply chain, and guarantee freshness and verified origin.

"We measure our success not simply in packages shipped, but in the smiles of grandparents receiving their hometown snacks, and families lighting authentic brass diyas thousands of miles from India."
